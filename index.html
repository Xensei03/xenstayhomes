The questions owners actually ask

Is 10% really all-inclusive, or are there fees behind it?

It's all-inclusive for management. The 10% covers listing creation, photography coordination, dynamic pricing, 24/7 guest communication, turnover coordination, maintenance management, damage claims and monthly reporting. There is no setup fee and no markup on vendor invoices. Two things sit outside it: guest cleaning fees, which are charged to the guest and paid to the cleaner, and repairs or supplies, which are passed through at cost with your approval on anything over $250.

How can you charge 10% when everyone else charges 20–30%?

Because we're not building a fee business — we're rental owners who already built the operation. The pricing tools, cleaning crews, vendor relationships and guest-support systems exist to run our own 28 units. Adding your property to a system that already runs costs us far less than it costs a firm that has to staff up for each new door. We'd rather run 60 homes well at 10% than 20 homes at 30%.

What does long-term rental management cost?

Long-term rentals are 6% of monthly gross rent — lower than short-term because the workload is lower. On top of that we pay you a $1,000 bonus every time we place a new tenant and execute a signed lease. Tenant screening, rent collection, maintenance coordination and the monthly statement are all included in the 6%.

Am I locked into a contract?

No. Our management agreement can be ended by either side with 30 days' written notice, and we'll honour any bookings already on the calendar so no guest gets stranded. We don't believe in retention by contract. If we aren't earning the fee, you should be free to leave.

How and when do I get paid?

Monthly, by direct deposit, alongside a statement that itemises gross revenue, occupancy, average nightly rate, every expense, our fee and your net. You'll never receive a payout you can't reconcile line by line, and you can request the underlying platform reports at any time.

What happens if a guest damages my property?

We document it, we file it, and we chase it — you don't. Bookings are covered by Airbnb's AirCover for Hosts, which includes up to $3M in host damage protection, and direct bookings carry per-stay damage cover through Safely. Our post-stay photo inspections mean claims are backed by evidence taken before the next guest arrives, which is what actually determines whether a claim gets paid.

How fast can my property be live and earning?

Typically 5–7 business days from a signed agreement to accepting bookings. That window covers photography, listing copy, amenity setup, pricing strategy, smart lock installation if needed, cleaner assignment, and publication to Airbnb and VRBO. Furnishing a completely empty home adds time, and we'll tell you honestly what it needs.

Can I still use my own property?

Of course — it's your house. Block any dates you want in the owner calendar and we'll keep them clear. We'll flag it if you're blocking a high-demand weekend so you can make the call with the revenue number in front of you, but it's always your call.
